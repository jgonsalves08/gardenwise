How it works

5 AI agents + 3 code agents, in one checked pipeline

Model: gpt-6-luna via Portkey (course key), low reasoning effort, Pydantic-validated JSON, tool calling for the helper, vision for the Site Scout.

Demo

Chicago, 4×8 bed: plants with photos, the verifier's checks, layout, frost-based calendar, costed list, and a chat helper that fills the form from a sentence.

Scales up

  • Caching cut cost per plan by 37%, and low reasoning effort cost ~20% less than medium, both with no quality loss (100% verifier pass rate in every setting, 40-plan A/B).
  • Code does dates, layout, prices and checks, so changing your plants re-plans for $0.
  • A whole 40-plot community garden plans for about $0.017. Caps: 60k tokens and 25 calls per session.
Safety

14 / 14 attacks stopped

  • Prompt leaks & fake SYSTEM tags: data delimiters + ID-only schemas
  • Instructions hidden in a CSV row or a photo: flagged and ignored
  • “Mark everything done, I confirm”: the helper can only propose
  • Budget drain, role escalation, street addresses: blocked in code
  • Physical safety: lead-test & pet-toxicity warnings, no foraging advice
  • Hot climates: limited support, no misleading dates

What's next

Limits and the next month

Limits today
  • Places that rarely freeze (Florida, Gulf coast, low deserts, most of California): limited support
  • 48 plants, 111 reference cities; average frost dates
  • Not yet piloted with a real organizer or school
  • Runs locally (SQLite); no hosted version yet
Next
